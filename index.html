About This Forum
🏛️

A Forum for Joppa and Joppatowne

This nonpartisan candidate forum is hosted by the Joppa Development and Heritage Corporation to give our community a direct opportunity to hear from the people running for office in our area. Every resident deserves to know who wants to represent them.
